# 📊 Mobile Sales Analysis – Key Questions

## This document outlines the core analytical questions to guide the evaluation of mobile sales data across brands, regions, and customer segments.

🔍 1. Top-Selling Brands and Models
Which mobile brands and models are the top sellers overall?
Which models perform best in specific countries or cities?
📱 2. Product Variants Analysis
How do sales numbers vary by:
Storage size
Color
Operating system (Android vs. iOS)
👥 3. Customer Profile Insights
What is the typical customer profile for different brands or models?
Age group
Gender
💰 4. Sales Channels & Payment Methods
How do sales and revenues break down across:
Online channels
Partner stores
In-store purchases
What are the preferred payment types?
🌍 5. Regional Pricing & Sales Trends
Are there noticeable differences in:
Pricing
Sales volume
across regions or cities?
🏆 6. Revenue & Volume Leaders
Which countries or cities generate:
The highest total revenue
The highest number of units sold?
📊 7. Demographic Patterns
Are there patterns in customer demographics based on:
Mobile brand
Model
Price range?
📅 8. Monthly Sales Trends (2024)
How does sales performance change month over month in 2024?
🔗 9. Age vs Device Preference Correlation
Are there correlations between customer age groups and the type of devices they purchase?
Example: Do younger customers prefer specific brands?
